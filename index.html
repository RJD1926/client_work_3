Our Sacred Heritage

The Sacred Art of Storytelling through Movement

At Shree Rukmamba Nrityalaya (SRN Bharatanatyam), dance is not merely a performance — it is a prayer in motion, an awakening of divine consciousness, and an unbroken lineage of Indian classical wisdom.

Under the guidance of our revered Gurus, disciples receive rigorous training in pristine footwork, symmetrical geometry, and expressive Navarasa nuances.

Nritta (Pure Dance)

Flawless rhythmic coordination of Adavus, Jathis, and Korvais in varying tempos.

Abhinaya (Expression)

Evocative portrayal of shlokas and padams through facial bhavas and mudras.

Natyashastra Theory

Comprehensive grasp of Tala, Shiro-bheda, Drishti-bheda, and Hasta Viniyogas.

Arangetram Solo

Complete Margam debut performance marking graduation into an accomplished artist.

Rigorous & Disciplined Training

Our Bharatanatyam Curriculum

A meticulously crafted syllabus spanning foundational postures to complex multi-speed Varnams and graduation repertoires.

Beginner

Ages 5+ • Foundation

Foundational Aramandi posture, fundamental Adavus (Tatta, Natta, Kuditta Mettu), Asamyuta & Samyuta Hasta mudras, basic Talam cycles, and Dhyana Shloka.

Intermediate

2-4 Years • Jathis & Margam

Complex Korvais, speed shifts (Prathama, Dwitiya, Tritiya kalam), introduction to Abhinaya and facial bhavas, Alarippu, Jatiswaram, and Shabdam compositions.

Advanced

4+ Years • Arangetram Stage

Full-length Varnams, intricate Padams, Javalis, Tillana, stage presence, theoretical treatises of Natyashastra, and solo Arangetram preparation.

Master Guidance

Specialized Workshops & Masterclasses

Immersive masterclasses guided by revered acharyas to hone specific dimensions of classical repertoire.
